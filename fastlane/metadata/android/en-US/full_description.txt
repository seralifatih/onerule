OneRule is an open-source offline password manager.

All data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted database powered by SQLCipher (AES-256). No account is required and no internet connection is needed.

Features

• Encrypted local database (SQLCipher, AES-256)
• Master PIN with optional biometric unlock
• Panic Mode (secondary PIN opens a separate decoy vault)
• Encrypted backup export and import
• Category filtering and search
• Multi-language support
• Dark and light theme

Panic Mode

A secondary PIN can be configured to open a separate decoy vault with harmless entries. This is intended for situations where you may be forced to unlock your device.

Privacy

OneRule does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising libraries. All data remains on the device. The source code is publicly available under the GPL-3.0 license.

Important

Your PIN is required to access your data. If it is forgotten, the encrypted data cannot be recovered.
